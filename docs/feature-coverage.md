## Remaining independent work

1. Complete slate-wide approval, measured own-post/bet evaluation, interactive brand interview/refresh, complete-archive semantic idea deduplication and broader market/format research. Durable steps, typed outputs, private draft/card artifacts, settings, scoped research and atomic recovery now exist. (`workflow.weekly`, `workflow.head`, `workflow.command`, `workflow.brand`, `workflow.idea`, `chat.deep_social`)
2. Add in-place table editing within documents, reusable templates and AI-to-typed-table creation; live previews, mentions, backlinks and paragraph suggestions now exist. (`tables.embed`)
3. Add grouped table/list presentation and broader embedded table views; typed semantics, row identities, ranges, saved views and calendar recurrence are implemented. (`tables.layouts`)
4. Connect feed UI to actual connector sync; add paginated resumable ingestion, hierarchy import and queued export artifacts/progress. (`library.rss`, `reader.readwise`, `library.import_notion`, `library.export`)
5. Add OCR/layout-preserving extraction and native PDF page/selection integration. Personal continuation, EPUB navigation, anchored highlights, browser narration and microphone capture are implemented. (`reader.articles`, `reader.pdf`)
6. Complete credit-based DM pause/resume and routine count/model/email options. Durable DM rate reservations and child-workflow overlap protection are now implemented and covered by five focused regressions. (`dm.delivery`, `routines.schedules`)
7. Rebuild edited skill bundle exports, load references on demand, persist visible activation and provide empty named knowledge slots on template installs. (`skills.portable`, `skills.activate`, `custom.sharing`)
8. Add own-baseline/period analysis, evidence-backed scheduling slots and richer platform-specific draft/media controls. (`analytics.insights`, `publish.calendar`, `publish.composer`)
9. Implement explicit entitlement/credit lifecycle and custom-domain verification/routing. Current quotas are operational allowances, not a subscription credit system. (`billing.plans`, `billing.credits`, `dm.domain`)
10. Build authorized corpus ingestion/identity/refresh and ad comparison/archive services; production data access and provider approvals are external dependencies. (`research.discover`, `research.creators`, `research.ads`, `research.brands`, `research.advariants`)
11. Build and verify dedicated authorized companion/import adapters. Generic visible-page capture/manual handoff does not complete these features. (`library.instagram_saves`, `publish.substack`, `reader.kindle`, `reader.snipd`, `extension.capture`)
